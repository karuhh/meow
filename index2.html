SUMMARY

The story follows a young boy named Thiều growing up in a rural Vietnamese village with his younger brother Tường. (read more...)

Through Thiều’s perspective, the book captures childhood memories filled with innocence, jealousy, love, and small everyday adventures. As Thiều struggles with feelings of rivalry toward his kind-hearted brother, he gradually learns about compassion, guilt, and maturity. Overall, the novel reflects the beauty and complexity of childhood against a simple countryside backdrop.
