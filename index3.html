HOW TO READ CODES

Have you ever struggled to find a book in the entire school library? Have you ever struggled to remember where the book you took out of the shelf used to be? Introduce: location codes, assigned to each book to fix the problem!

Take a look at the library map!

You might be able to figure it out from here!

"S" and "T"

- stands for
"students" and "teachers",
meaning students' area. "T" stands for "Teachers' area".

Letters

- represents the designated location of the bookshelves in each area!

Numbers

Look at this model. Letters represent the row of bookshelf - where the book is.
For example, "1" means the first row of the bookshelf from up to down, "2" means second and so on.
